**Elevation Finance** — Last updated: June 8, 2026

## Overview

Elevation Finance is a financial calculator app for iOS that explains its information handling practices through this privacy policy.

## Information We Collect

The app states: "We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data." All calculations occur locally on the device without server transmission.

## Advertising

The application integrates Google AdMob for ad delivery. Google may collect device identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location data, and app usage information to serve ads. This data collection is handled by Google rather than the app developers.

## App Tracking Transparency

On iOS 14 and later, users receive tracking permission requests. Users can deny permission while maintaining full app functionality with non-personalized advertisements. Ad preferences are manageable through iOS Settings and Google Ad Settings.

## Children's Privacy

The app is not designed for children under 13, and the developers state they do not knowingly collect data from this age group.

## Third-Party Services

Google AdMob serves as the sole third-party service, handling advertising functions.

## Changes to This Policy

The developers reserve the right to update this policy, with continued app use implying acceptance of modifications.
